## TIL

Today, during my commute, I attended lectures on R Programming and Digital Logic Circuits.  

In R Programming, I learned about the use of tibbles. Although the topic itself was not particularly difficult, I came to understand a tibble as a more modern and user-friendly version of the traditional data frame. From my perspective, if offers far more advantages, so I expect that I will use tibbles more frequently than conventional data frames in the future.  

In Digital Logic Circuits, I listened to the final lecture on combinational logic circuits. However, I found the lecture consistently difficult to follow bacause the instructor only provided verbal explanations without working through actual calculations. After about thirty minutes, I realized that continuing in the same way would not be effective, so I stopped the lecture.  

Instead, I found a much better lecture on Digital Logic Circuits, and I have decided to follow that Youtube course rather than rely solely on my university lecture from now on. Since I felt that I could be in serious trouble by the time of the final examination if I continued like this, I decided to take a decisive step and change my study strategy.
