## TIL

Today's schedule emcompassed both physical training and academic study.  

For my workout, I completed a push-and-pull resistance routine that included exercises such as long pulls, chest presses, inner chest presses, lat pulldowns with a MAC grip, and one-arm seated rows.  

Academically, I focused on two main subjects: Digital Logic Circuits and Introduction to Statistics.  
The statistics lecture covered Part 1 of probability distributions. Beginning with this section, a growing number of concepts were introduced, which made the material increasingly confusing. Therefore, I turned to a Youtube lecture for additional explanation, and it was quite helpful.  

Rather than continuing with a new Digital Logic Circuits lecture, I spent time reviewing the concepts I had failed to understand the previous day by searching for multiple explanations on Youtube. In particular, I studied Karnaugh maps as well as NAND and NOR gates. At this point, I feel that I have finally developed a basic understanding of these topics. However, understanding the concepts themselves and applying them to solve problems are entirely different matters. For that reason, I'm trying to build intuition by solving as many practice problems as possible.
